## How to locate evidence

Evidence locations refer to [Raqmi_Capstone.ipynb](Raqmi_Capstone.ipynb). Cell numbers below are **zero-based indexes in the uploaded executed notebook**. Stable cell IDs and visible section names are also supplied because subsequently appended verification cells do not belong to the historical run. Historical outputs describe the uploaded Colab execution; a new local verification does not become a live-provider rerun.

**Status meanings:** implemented/captured means code and matching executed evidence exist; partial means only part of the requirement is evidenced; gap means the required evidence is absent. Deterministic scaffolds are identified explicitly.

Finalization makes live execution explicit through `ENABLE_LIVE_BACKENDS=False` by default and `RUN_LIVE_GOLDEN = ENABLE_LIVE_BACKENDS`. The historic outputs remain preserved; [source_manifest.json](evidence/source_manifest.json) records provenance and the configuration edits. The supplemental [native-tool module](raqmi_tool_calling.py) and [its tests](tests/test_tool_calling.py) are an **offline protocol extension, not integrated with the captured `ask()`/evaluation; provider verification is missing**. Neither addition retroactively upgrades the historical results to native function-calling evidence.

## Additional course items

The rubric lists peer architecture sign-off, logged peer attacks and other Day 4 milestones. The uploaded notebook does not establish independent peer sign-off or a peer red-team record. These cannot be fabricated during repository packaging.

Extensions are optional bonus work, conditioned on mandatory-scope performance. The historical notebook does not demonstrate one of the named extensions; no extension credit is claimed. A native-tool compliance addition is engineering work on the mandatory tools requirement, not automatically one of those named extensions.

The rubric's critical submission rule is that a red safety suite cannot pass. The other explicit capping risks are altered Golden expectations to hide failures, guard rates without their false-positive pair, savings without an adjacent evaluation verdict, and an uncalibrated judge controlling a gate. The historical gate is deterministic and does **not** use the synthetic judge scores.
